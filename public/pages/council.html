How to play

  1. The kingdom has 🪙 5 Gold and 👥 5 People.
  2. Each round a problem comes up. Pay 1 Gold or lose 1 People.
  3. One answer is a secret trap. Some phones get a whisper saying which.
  4. The leader picks an answer and one partner. Everyone votes.
  5. The two secretly Help or Sabotage.
  6. Survive 5 rounds: loyal wins. Hit 0: traitors win.

Who you are

  • Loyal Keep the kingdom alive.
  • Traitor Drain it to zero. Don't get caught.
  • Whisper A secret tip about the trap.
  • Leader Picks the plan each round.
