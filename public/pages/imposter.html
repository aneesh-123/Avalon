Play Imposter Online

Everyone knows the secret word except one. Find the imposter.

Play now
  • 4–15 players
  • 10–20 min
  • One phone or many

How to play

  1. Make a room and share the code, or pass one phone around the table.
  2. Everyone sees the same secret word, except the Imposter, who only sees the category.
  3. Take turns saying one word that hints at the secret word. Not too obvious, or the imposter will catch on.
  4. Discuss, then vote on who you think the imposter is.
  5. Catch them and the players win, unless the imposter guesses the word and steals the win.

Extra roles

  • Detective knows the word and one player who is definitely innocent.
  • Jester wins alone by getting voted out.
  • More than one imposter for big groups.
  • Custom words: the host types their own secret word.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. No app and no account.
Can we play with one phone?
Yes. Pass-and-play mode shows each player their word in turn.
What words are there?
Lots of categories, or type your own.
Is this like Spyfall?
Similar idea: one player doesn't know the secret and has to blend in.
