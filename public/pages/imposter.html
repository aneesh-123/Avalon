Imposter

Everyone knows the word, except one

Play now How to play
  • 4–15 players
  • Free
  • One phone or many

How to play

  1. Make a room, or pass one phone around.
  2. Everyone sees the secret word. The Imposter only sees the category.
  3. Each player says one clue word.
  4. Vote on who the imposter is.
  5. Catch them to win. But if the imposter guesses the word, they win.

Extra roles

  • Detective Knows one innocent player.
  • Jester Wins if voted out.
  • More imposters For big groups.
  • Custom words Type your own.

Good to know

Is it free?
Yes. No app and no account.
One phone?
Yes. Pass-and-play mode works.
