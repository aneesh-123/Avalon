Trivia Night with Phone Buzzers

Teams, buzzers and bragging rights. Every phone is a buzzer.

Play now
  • 2+ teams
  • Host or no host
  • Free, no sign-up

How to play

  1. Make a room and pick categories. Teams join on their phones with the code.
  2. With a host: you read the questions aloud and run the buzzer. Everyone else buzzes in.
  3. No host: questions and choices appear on every phone, and the app keeps score.
  4. Fastest correct team gets the points. Buzz order is fair: it's timed on each phone, not by internet speed.
  5. Highest score at the end wins.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. No app and no account.
How many people can play?
Two or more teams, any size.
Do I need a host?
No. Pick "No host" and the app runs it.
Is it good for a party or classroom?
Yes. Put the host screen on a TV and play from phones.
