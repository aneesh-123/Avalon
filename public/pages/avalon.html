How to play

  1. Make a room. Friends join on their phones.
  2. Your phone shows your secret role: Good or Evil.
  3. The leader picks a team. Everyone votes.
  4. The team plays Pass or Fail in secret.
  5. 3 passed quests: Good wins. 3 failed: Evil wins.
  6. Evil gets one last chance: find Merlin.

Special roles

  • Merlin Knows Evil. Must stay hidden.
  • Percival Knows Merlin.
  • Morgana Pretends to be Merlin.
  • Mordred Hidden from Merlin.
  • Oberon Evil, but alone.
  • Lady of the Lake Checks one player.
