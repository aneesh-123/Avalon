Play Avalon Online

The hidden-role game of loyalty and betrayal. Free, on your phones.

Play now
  • 5+ players
  • 30–60 min
  • Free, no sign-up

How to play

  1. One person makes a room and shares the code or link. Everyone joins on their own phone.
  2. Each phone secretly shows your role. Most players are Good, a few are Evil, and Evil knows each other.
  3. Each round a leader picks a team for a quest. Everyone votes to approve or reject the team.
  4. The team secretly plays Pass or Fail. One Fail sinks the quest.
  5. Three successful quests and Good is close to winning. Three failed quests and Evil wins.
  6. If Good wins three quests, the Assassin gets one guess at Merlin. Guess right and Evil steals the win.

Roles you can add

  • Merlin knows who is Evil but must stay hidden.
  • Percival knows who Merlin is, or thinks he does.
  • Morgana pretends to be Merlin.
  • Mordred is Evil but hidden from Merlin.
  • Oberon is Evil but doesn't know the other Evil players.
  • Lady of the Lake lets a player check someone's loyalty.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. No app to download and no account.
Do we need to be in the same room?
It works best around a table or on a video call. Every phone shows only its own secret role.
How many players do we need?
At least 5.
Is there a tutorial?
Yes. Tap Avalon and there's a short walkthrough plus an "Ask a question" helper for rules.
